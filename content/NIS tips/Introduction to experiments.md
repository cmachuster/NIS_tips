Starting with NIS 6 a new way to control the system and to design acquisition is introduced, **the Experiments**.

<img src="Experiments - 1 - Annotated.png" width="400">

The idea behind experiments is to make life easier to navigate between channels and to set them.

Effectively the channel created are all displayed together and the values for the illumination and the exposure time for each of them is easily accessible and adjustable.

>[!note] Depending on the configuration, the account, the customer and the complexity of the acquisition one might still need to use OCs (more options for customization available), so they are kept available.

## Creating an Experiment configuration

The logic of Experiment creation is the same as for the AX.

- Click on « Exp. Settings » next to the experiment menu

<img src="Experiments - 1 - Crop.png" width="400">


- A list of the existing Experiment set up is displayed. To create a new one click on « ➕Add »


<img src="Experiments - 2 - annotated.png" width="400">

- you will be able to automatically setup configuration by typing the name of the dye that is to be associated with the channel

<img src="Experiments - 3 - annotated.png" width="400">

>[!warning] It is EXTREMELY important to declare correctly the excitation and emission filters as well as the DM otherwise, NIS will configure Experiment the wrong way or even **==won’t be able to create them==**

- An experiment is proposed and will be possible to adjust them (if needed) by going in Edit

<img src="Experiments - 4 - annotated.png" width="400">

- and change the false color associated with the channel and its name. It is possible to go further by going in Expert Mode

<img src="Experiments - 5 - annotated.png" width="400">


- In Expert Mode all the modifications are available : the false colors, the channel names, the light source and the filter cube

<img src="Experiments - 6 - annotated.png" width="400">

- everything is set to satisfaction end the setup by the Saving the Experiment and giving it a name
