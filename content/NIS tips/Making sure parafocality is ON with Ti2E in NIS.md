Per default for Ti2E, even if the parafocality settings are done in Ti2Control they will not be applied when changing objectives in NIS.

To make sure it does, open the Ti2 configuration window (in the Ti2E « Configure » button within the Ti2E Control tab or from Device manager) and make sure the « Allow Parfocal Correction » is ticked
