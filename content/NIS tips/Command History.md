It is possible to use the Command History window to see some of the last command executed.

<img src="Command History Menu.png" width="300">

NIS displays most of commands corresponding to the last action executed.

<img src="Command History.png" width="400">

here you can see the command for changing OCs on line 9 : `SelectOptConf(xxx);` or changing LightPath on line 10 `SelectLighPath(xxx);`

The last used command can also be visualized on the bottom left corner of NIS.

<img src="NIS tips - 3.png" width="400">

>[!tip] By double clicking it you will open the Command History window.
